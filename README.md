# Google AI Essentials

This repository contains my summaries from the **Google AI Essentials** specialization, completed via Coursera under the Google Career Certificates program. The specialization covers the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI, applied prompting skills, responsible AI use, and how to stay current as AI tools evolve, with a focus on real-world application in professional workflows.

---

## About the Specialization

The specialization consists of **5 modules**, each building toward a practical, workplace-ready understanding of AI, not just the theory, but how to apply it to everyday tasks, decision-making, and process improvement.

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- What AI is (and isn't), and how machine learning and generative AI actually work under the hood
- How to use AI tools to remove repetitive work and speed up documentation, summarization, and analysis
- How to write structured, effective prompts, a skill that maps directly onto writing clear business requirements
- How to recognize bias, privacy risk, and the limits of AI output, and build responsible human-in-the-loop checkpoints
- How to keep building AI fluency as tools evolve, and use it as a career differentiator

## Why This Repo Matters for Business Analyst / Data Analyst Work

AI literacy is becoming a baseline expectation for both **Business Analyst** and **Data Analyst** roles, not as a separate technical skill, but as a layer on top of existing requirements-writing, data-analysis, and process-mapping work. Each module summary in this repo explicitly connects the concept back to:

- **Business Analysis:** requirement writing, BRD/FRD documentation, acceptance criteria, and process mapping for AI-enabled features
- **Data Analysis:** data quality practices, sense-checking AI-assisted analysis, and turning findings into clear narratives
- **Automation:** identifying where AI/automation fits into a business process, and documenting the human-oversight checkpoints that responsible automation requires
